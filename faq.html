Frequently asked questions

What is GTA VI Clips?

A desktop tool that cuts short highlight clips from GTA VI gameplay recordings and publishes them to the creator's own TikTok account and YouTube channel.

Whose content is published?

Only gameplay recorded by the creator who uses the tool. GTA VI Clips is not used to repost other people's videos.

Which TikTok permissions does it request, and why?

user.info.basic to show which account is connected, video.publish to post approved clips to that account, and video.upload to send a clip to TikTok drafts instead. No other TikTok data is requested.

Is anything posted without my approval?

No. Only clips the creator has reviewed and moved to the approved folder are ever published, and the privacy level is chosen in the posting step.

Where is my data stored?

On your own computer. Access tokens are saved in a local file readable only by your user account. GTA VI Clips has no server and no database. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I disconnect TikTok or YouTube?

On TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services (Manage app permissions) > remove GTA VI Clips. On Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can also delete the local token file at any time.

Is GTA VI Clips affiliated with Rockstar Games, TikTok or Google?

No. Grand Theft Auto and GTA are trademarks of Take-Two Interactive Software. TikTok and YouTube are trademarks of their owners. GTA VI Clips is an independent project.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. GTA VI Clips is free and has no ads or in-app purchases.
