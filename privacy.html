Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GTA VI Clips ("GTA VI Clips", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal data when you use the GTA VI Clips desktop tool and this website (together, the "Service"), and the rights you have over that data. By using the Service you acknowledge this Policy.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Gabin, the independent creator who operates GTA VI Clips, based in France. Contact: albinetgabin@gmail.com.

We do not collect your TikTok password, your followers, your messages, your viewing history, your contacts, your location or any data about other TikTok users.

3. How we use your data

  • To sign you in and show which TikTok account is connected, so clips go to the right account.
  • To publish the clips you have approved to your TikTok account (video.publish) or send them to your TikTok drafts (video.upload), and to your YouTube channel.
  • To keep a local log of what was published so a clip is never posted twice.
  • To answer your messages and requests.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, selling, or training machine-learning models.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

We process your data to provide the Service you asked for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), on the basis of your consent given on the TikTok and Google authorization screens (Article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time, and for our legitimate interest in keeping the Service secure (Article 6(1)(f)).

5. Where your data is stored

GTA VI Clips runs on your own computer. Tokens, logs and videos are stored locally on that computer, in files readable only by your user account. We do not operate a server or a database and we do not receive a copy of your data.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. Data is only sent to:

  • TikTok, to authorize the Service and publish your videos, under the TikTok Privacy Policy;
  • Google / YouTube, to upload your videos, under the Google Privacy Policy;
  • authorities, only where required by law.

These platforms may process data outside the European Economic Area under their own safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).

7. How long we keep data

  • Access and refresh tokens: until you disconnect the Service or they expire, then deleted.
  • Publishing log: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Emails: up to 12 months after the conversation ends.

Videos you publish remain on TikTok and YouTube until you delete them there.

8. Security

Tokens are stored with file permissions that only allow your user account to read them, all connections to TikTok and Google use HTTPS, and only the minimum scopes needed are requested. No method is 100% secure; if a breach affecting your data occurs, we will notify you and the competent authority as required by law.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to the processing of, and receive a portable copy of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email albinetgabin@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL, or your local authority. Residents of California and other jurisdictions have equivalent rights under local law, including the right not to have their data sold (we never sell data).

10. Disconnecting and deleting your data

  • TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services (Manage app permissions) > remove GTA VI Clips.
  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions > remove GTA VI Clips.
  • Delete the local token and log files, or uninstall the tool, to erase all locally stored data immediately.

11. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required to use TikTok in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has used the Service, contact us and we will delete the data.

12. YouTube API Services and Google data

GTA VI Clips uses YouTube API Services. By using GTA VI Clips you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access at Google security settings.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. The effective date above shows the latest version; significant changes will be announced on this website before they take effect.
