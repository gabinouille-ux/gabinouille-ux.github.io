GTA VI Clips

GTA VI Clips is a desktop tool that turns long GTA VI gameplay recordings into short, subtitled vertical clips, and publishes them to the creator's own TikTok account and YouTube channel on a schedule.

See how it worksRead the FAQ

Features

Automatic highlight detection

Scans a gameplay recording and finds the most intense moments (action, chases, reactions) using audio and motion analysis.

Vertical 9:16 clips

Cuts 30 to 60 second clips and reframes them to 1080×1920 for TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

Subtitles and titles

Generates subtitles from the voice track and adds a short title to each clip, with a caption and hashtags ready to post.

Review before posting

Nothing is published automatically until the creator has watched the clip and moved it to the approved folder.

Scheduled publishing

Publishes one approved clip at 12:00 and 18:00 (Paris time) to the creator's TikTok account and YouTube channel.

Private by design

Runs on the creator's own computer. Videos and access tokens stay on that computer; there is no server or database.

How it works

  1. Record. The creator records their own GTA VI gameplay and drops the file into the gameplay to process folder.
  2. Clip. The tool detects the best moments, cuts vertical clips, adds subtitles and a title, and places them in the clips to review folder.
  3. Review. The creator watches each clip and moves the ones they want to publish to the approved folder.
  4. Connect TikTok. The first time, the creator clicks Log in with TikTok in the tool. TikTok asks them to authorize GTA VI Clips (Login Kit, scope user.info.basic); the tool shows the connected account's display name and avatar so they can check it is the right account.
  5. Publish. At each scheduled time, the tool sends one approved clip with its caption to the creator's TikTok account through the Content Posting API (video.publish for direct posting, or video.upload to send it to TikTok drafts for final editing). The creator chooses the privacy level shown by TikTok before posting.
  6. Track. Published clips move to the sent folder and are recorded in a local log, so a clip is never posted twice.

GTA VI Clips only ever posts to the account of the person who authorized it. It does not read, collect or post anything on other people's accounts.

YouTube integration

The same approved clips are uploaded as YouTube Shorts to the creator's own channel through the YouTube Data API. GTA VI Clips uses YouTube API Services: see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
