GTA VI Clips

GTA VI Clips is a personal tool run by a single creator. It cuts short highlight clips from the creator's own GTA VI gameplay recordings, adds a title and subtitles, and publishes them to the creator's own TikTok account and YouTube channel.

How it works

  1. The creator records gameplay and drops it in a folder on their computer.
  2. The tool detects the best moments and cuts 60-second vertical clips.
  3. The creator reviews the clips and keeps the ones they want.
  4. Twice a day, the tool uploads one approved clip, with its caption and hashtags, to the creator's own TikTok account (Content Posting API) and YouTube channel (YouTube Data API).

The tool is only used by its owner, on their own accounts. It does not collect data from other people.
